Section 01

Executive Summary

Real-time shop floor visibility, instant failure alerts, and automated stock control

The Thermo-GMAO platform is a custom-engineered Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) designed explicitly to digitize, streamline, and optimize maintenance and operations on our injection molding factory floor.

By shifting from passive, fragmented paperwork to a proactive digital ecosystem, Thermo-GMAO connects physical assets with operations staff. It bridges the gap between field technicians scanning machine QR codes for rapid feedback and plant management navigating a **live 3D Digital Twin** of the plant floor. The platform drives down the Mean Time to Repair (MTTR), optimizes spare parts inventory levels automatically, and tracks machine efficiency metrics shift-by-shift.

"Thermo-GMAO transforms shop floor maintenance from a reactive emergency service into a structured, predictive utility — guaranteeing maximum machine availability and transparent audit logs."

Key Modules & Technical Capabilities

A deep dive into the engineering behind our smart features

4.1 Live 3D Factory Digital Twin

A WebGL-powered spatial digital twin of the factory floor allows managers to quickly locate failures. Machines glow dynamically according to their real-time state:

Operational — Healthy Machine running current product
Down — Unplanned breakdown requiring urgent technician scan
Maintenance — Planned preventive task in-progress
Retired / Standby — Asset offline or awaiting configuration

4.3 Industrial Maintenance Metrics & Formulas

Our platform handles critical metrics reporting to keep the shop floor competitive. Automated computation boxes calculate results continuously:

Mean Time to Repair (MTTR)
MTTR = Total DownTime ÷ Failure Count
Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)
MTBF = Total Operating Time ÷ Failure Count
Taux de Rendement Synthétique (TRS / OEE)
TRS (%) = (Actual Qty × Actual Cycle Time) ÷ (Total Operating Time × Cavities Running) × 100%
Section 05

Database Architecture & Specs

Robust, local relational engine running SQLite for high reliability

Thermo-GMAO is powered by a high-efficiency database schema in SQLite (`better-sqlite3`). It runs 100% locally on the industrial control PC or local intranet server, guaranteeing data residency compliance and zero maintenance interruptions during external internet outages.

Table Name Primary Columns Functional Purpose
users uid, username, password, displayName, role Contains encrypted user profiles and active RBAC clearance roles.
machines id, name, serialNumber, clampingForce, status, currentHours, injectingProduct, imageUrl Tracks technical injection molding machine specifications, state, and hours.
work_orders id, machineId, type, priority, status, assignedTo, completedAt, reportNumber Core system logs for open corrective tickets and preventive intervention tasks.
intervention_reports workOrderId, technicians, startTime, endTime, durationMinutes, failureCause, actions Detailed diagnosis log filed by technicians upon closing a work order.
spare_parts id, name, sku, category, location, stock, minStock, unit Live inventory database mapping items, quantities, and storage coordinates.
intervention_parts id, workOrderId, partId, quantity Junction ledger tracking precisely which spare parts were consumed where.
audit_logs id, userId, username, action, entityType, entityId, details, createdAt Cryptographic logging system tracking all database writes, logins, and scans.
machine_rendement id, date, machineNumber, item, targetQty, actualCavitiesRunning, trs, actualCycleTime Stores the raw production and injection efficiency figures shift by shift.
🔒 Strict Data Residency Compliance: The database and server execute entirely inside the factory local area network (LAN). No analytical statistics or asset configurations are ever transmitted to external cloud systems, maintaining 100% security boundaries.

Deployment & Implementation Plan

Phased shop floor deployment ensuring zero operational disruption

Phase 01
Audit, Tagging & Database Import
Generate unique high-durability physical QR labels for all shop floor machinery and spare part bins. Import standard injection molding machine cadence rates, clamping force ratings, and core inventory lists into SQLite.
Weeks 1–2
Phase 02
Pilot Testing & 3D Calibration
Deploy the local server. Configure user accounts and test the 3-second QR scan flow on 3 pilot machines. Align the live 3D factory layout nodes with actual factory coords.
Weeks 3–4
Phase 03
Technician Onboarding & Launch
Conduct hands-on, thumb-friendly training sessions for technicians. Decommission paper breakdown logbooks. Begin using the purchase request generator for low-stock spare parts.
Weeks 5–6
Phase 04
Continuous Optimization & TRS Review
Analyze compiled MTTR/MTBF metrics in weekly review meetings. Leverage automated machine rendement reports to adjust machine targets and scheduling.
Ongoing
