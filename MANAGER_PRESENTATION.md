# 🚀 Modernizing Maintenance: The Thermo-GMAO Platform

**Executive Summary**  
Currently, our factory floor maintenance relies on delayed reporting, fragmented machine histories, and a lack of real-time visibility. Thermo-GMAO is a custom-built, modern Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) designed specifically for our operations to eliminate downtime, digitize tracking, and keep our production lines moving.

---

## 🏭 Why We Need It: The Value Proposition

1. **Zero-Friction Mobile Updates (QR Code Integration)**
   - **Problem:** Technicians waste time logging into complex systems to report broken machines.
   - **Solution:** Every machine gets a unique QR code. A technician scans it with their phone, logs the machine as `Down`, and closes the app in exactly 3 seconds.
   - **ROI:** Mean Time to Repair (MTTR) is significantly reduced because alerts are instant and frictionless.

2. **Real-time 3D Factory Layout Visibility**
   - **Problem:** Managers lack an eagle-eye view of the factory floor's real-time operational status.
   - **Solution:** Thermo-GMAO features an immersive 3D digital twin of our factory. Machines glow red if they crash, yellow if under maintenance, and green if operational.
   - **ROI:** Shift managers can identify bottlenecks at a glance without having to physically walk the floor.

3. **Data-Driven Part Inventory & Consumption**
   - **Problem:** Spare parts run out unexpectedly, halting production.
   - **Solution:** The platform tracks spare part usage dynamically against Work Orders and alerts us when stock hits the minimum threshold. 
   - **ROI:** Prevents extended downtime caused by missing crucial parts like heaters and sensors.

4. **Detailed Audit Logging & Security**
   - **Problem:** No traceability on who marked a machine as fixed or broken.
   - **Solution:** Fully integrated Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Every status change, whether from the dashboard or mobile QR, is cryptographically tied to the active user in the Audit Log.
